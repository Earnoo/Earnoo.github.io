About Alireza Jahani (علیرضا جهانی)

Alireza Jahani is an Applied Scientist and Machine Learning Researcher at the ARAS AI Lab (Advanced Robotics and Automated Systems) at K. N. Toosi University of Technology, under the supervision of Prof. Hamid D. Taghirad. He is an applicant for Fall 2026 / Fall 2027 PhD positions in Computer Science, Machine Learning, and Robotics.

Key Research Areas & Inventions

  • FTS-GAN: Fuzzy Temporal Segmentation Generative Adversarial Network for Retail and Multivariate Time Series (Under Review at Expert Systems with Applications, Elsevier).
  • EP-RNN: Event-Preserving Recurrent Neural Networks for robust sequential forecasting under irregular sampling and abrupt state transitions.
  • Fuzzy Clustering & Preprocessing: Novel sequential time-series segmentation and deep clustering architectures.
  • Multi-Agent Robotics & Mechatronics: Distributed coordination and autonomous control systems.
